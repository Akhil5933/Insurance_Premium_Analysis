# 📊 Insurance Premium Analysis Dashboard (Power BI)

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end Power BI dashboard built to analyze insurance business performance across policies, customers, agents, and regions.

The dashboard helps identify key trends in premium collection, profitability, and return on investment (ROI), enabling data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

* Analyze overall insurance premium performance
* Identify revenue leakage and collection gaps
* Evaluate profitability and underwriting efficiency
* Track policy and agent performance

---

## 📌 Key KPIs

* 📊 Total Policies: **7,299**
* 💰 Total Premium Amount: **39.09M**
* 💵 Total Premium Paid: **10.68M**
* 📈 Total Premium Payable: **28.41M**
* 📉 Underwriting Expenses: **38.66M**
* 📊 Total Annual Premium: **2.16M**
* 📉 % Premium Paid: **32.52%**
* 📈 % Premium Payable: **67.48%**

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 💡 1. Revenue vs Cost Efficiency

* Underwriting expenses are almost equal to total premium
* Indicates **low profit margins and cost inefficiency**

---

### ⚠️ 2. Premium Collection Gap

* Only **32.5% of premium is paid**
* Majority remains unpaid → potential **cash flow risk**

---

### 📊 3. Policy Performance

* **Whole Life policies (~43%)** contribute the highest returns
* Key revenue-driving product segment

---

### 📈 4. ROI Trends

* ROI peaks at ~19% but declines over time
* Indicates **inconsistent return performance**

---

### 🧑‍💼 5. Sales Agent Analysis

* Revenue contribution varies significantly across agents
* Opportunity for **performance optimization and training**

---

### 🌍 6. Regional Analysis

* Business is evenly distributed across states
* No heavy dependency on a single region

---

### 👨‍💻 7. Customer Segmentation

* High contribution from:

  * IT Professionals
  * Retail Buyers
  * Skilled Occupations

---

### 📉 8. Yearly Trend

* Peak performance observed around 2018
* Gradual decline in subsequent years

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

* Improve premium collection strategies
* Optimize underwriting costs
* Focus on high-performing policy types
* Monitor and enhance sales agent performance
* Implement data-driven decision strategies

---

## 📊 Dashboard Features

* Interactive filters (Policy Type, Year, State, Agent, Occupation)
* KPI Cards for quick insights
* Trend Analysis (Year-wise performance)
* Policy & Customer Segmentation
* Agent Performance Tracking
